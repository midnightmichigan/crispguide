About CrispGuide

Published as a faceless research publication · Methodology below is our complete commitment

What this site is

CrispGuide is a faceless, research-based guide publisher focused on air fryers and countertop cooking. There are no bylines, no author photos, and no personalities here — deliberately. The work stands on its sourcing, not on a persona.

We chose air fryers because the category has genuine, verifiable consumer demand. Market research firms report the global air fryer market at roughly USD 7–10 billion in 2025, growing at about 8–9% annually through the next decade (sources: Future Market Insights, Fortune Business Insights). The residential segment dominates — one forecast puts household share at about 88% of units in 2026 — meaning the audience is overwhelmingly everyday home cooks, which is exactly who our guides are written for.

What we do — and what we never do

We do:

  • Research from primary sources. Manufacturer spec sheets, published wattage and capacity data, verified buyer-rating distributions, and published cooking guidance (times, temperatures, safety rules).
  • Cite our evidence. When a guide states a fact — a market figure, a temperature rule, a sizing guideline — it states where the fact comes from or presents it as widely published guidance.
  • Say when we don't know. If buyer feedback is split or data is thin, we say so instead of inventing a verdict.
  • Correct ourselves. If a spec changes or guidance is updated, we update the article.

We never:

  • Claim hands-on testing we didn't do. We do not own, operate, or test air fryers in a lab. You will never read "we tested," "in our kitchen," or "after two weeks of frying" on this site — because it wouldn't be true.
  • Invent statistics. Numbers on this site come from named, published sources or are presented as clearly-labeled approximations (e.g., "a rough rule of thumb").
  • Fabricate buyer quotes or reviews. When we describe buyer-feedback patterns, we describe them in aggregate ("a recurring complaint across hundreds of ratings") — never as invented individual voices.
  • Accept payment for placement. No brand can buy a recommendation here. We currently have zero advertiser relationships and zero affiliate memberships.

How our picks are made

When a guide names a category pick (e.g., "best for families of 4"), the recommendation rests on verifiable criteria: published capacity, published feature set (dual-basket, preset programs, dishwasher-safe parts), price band from publicly listed retail prices at time of writing, and aggregate buyer-rating patterns (rating distribution across thousands of verified purchases). Each pick explains its reasoning in the article so you can evaluate it yourself.

Affiliate links: the current state

There are none. The "Check price" buttons throughout this site are explicitly labeled placeholders with no destination. We have not joined any affiliate program (Amazon Associates or otherwise). If that changes, every affiliate link will be labeled as such, and this page will be updated first — before any link goes live.

Safety note

Air fryers are high-wattage heating appliances (typically 1,200–1,800 watts). Our guides repeat the same safety basics every appliance manufacturer publishes: never leave a running fryer unattended for long, keep it on a heat-safe surface with clearance around the vents, and never submerge the electrical base in water. This site's content is informational; your appliance's manual is the authority on safe use.
